1. Cap Promotional Discounts at 20% to Prevent $125K in Capital Dissipation
OBSERVATION: Orders discounted >30% generated -$125,007 in net losses across 965 transactions. WHY IT MATTERS: Sales reps discount to hit top-line targets, evaporating 43.6% of enterprise net profit. RECOMMENDED ACTION: Institute ERP discount authorization gates: >20% requires VP Finance approval.
2. Restructure Furniture Category Fulfillment to Solve the $742K Deficit
OBSERVATION: Furniture drives $742K (32.3% of sales) but delivers only 2.49% profit margin ($18.5K). WHY IT MATTERS: Bulky Tables (-$17.7K loss) and Bookcases (-$3.5K loss) tie up freight and warehouse space. RECOMMENDED ACTION: Shift to made-to-order supplier drop-shipping and pass shipping surcharges directly to clients.
3. Scale Technology "Crown Jewels" (Copiers & IT Peripherals)
OBSERVATION: Copiers delivered $55.6K profit with a 37.2% margin; Phones achieved $330K sales and $44.5K profit. WHY IT MATTERS: B2B Technology has strong pricing power and corporate demand inelasticity. RECOMMENDED ACTION: Launch Managed Print Services and bundle hardware copiers with consumable paper/accessories.
4. Realize Regional Pricing Discipline in Central Territory & Texas
OBSERVATION: Texas lost -$25.7K on $170K sales due to 37.0% average discounting; Central margin is only 7.9%. WHY IT MATTERS: Excessive promotional discounting in Central territories undermines company profitability. RECOMMENDED ACTION: Realign Central sales commissions from gross revenue to Gross Margin Commission (GMC).
5. Unlock "Hidden Gems": Cross-Sell High-Margin Consumables
OBSERVATION: Paper delivers 43.4% margin ($34.1K profit); Envelopes and Labels both exceed 42% margin. WHY IT MATTERS: Zero price sensitivity, lightweight freight, and rapid commercial inventory turnover. RECOMMENDED ACTION: Add automated 1-click cart cross-selling and recurring B2B consumable subscriptions.
